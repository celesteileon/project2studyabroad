The tier list.

A personal ranking of the destinations I visited during my Spring 2026 semester abroad. This is about my individual experience and the itinerary I created for each of my trips.

For the full details behind these rankings, including my itineraries and travel notes, view my complete Madrid travel document.

View my Madrid Travel Doc!

The Tier Hierarchy

  • S TIER The elite, best-in-class, or must-have choice.
  • A TIER Excellent and high quality, just a step below S.
  • B TIER Good and solid, though not extraordinary.
  • C TIER Average or middle-of-the-pack.
  • D TIER Below average and generally avoided.
  • F TIER Failing or the absolute worst.
S to F

How I rank a place.

ItalyS TIER

Lake Como

Mar 31 through Apr 4

My solo trip, planned entirely by me during my spring break or otherwise known as, "semana santa." I stayed in Menaggio. I explored Bellagio, Varenna, Villa del Balbianello, Tremezzo, and a final stop at the Milan Cathedral. Absolutely beautiful.

MoroccoS TIER

Marrakesh and Sahara Desert

Apr 15 through 19

A City Life Madrid tour through the Atlas Mountains, Ait Ben Haddou, and Todra Canyon to a Berber camp, with camels, sandboarding, and a sunrise dune climb. Our guide Adam was awesome. Best decision ever.

SpainA TIER

Barcelona

Jan 30 through Feb 1

Sagrada Família (100% worth it), Casa Batlló, Casa Milà, Barceloneta Beach, and the Raluy circus, literally the best circus ever.

SpainA TIER

Madrid

Home base · Jan through May

Madrid became my everyday life and home abroad, from coffee at Monkee Koffee and La Postalera to the best dinner at Swiss Butter.

EnglandB TIER

London

Feb 26 through Mar 2

A long weekend that was all about the food. In between meals I saw the Tower of London, Borough Market, London Bridge, Big Ben, and Buckingham Palace, then spent the nights at The Roxy.

PortugalB TIER

Porto

Feb 20 through 22

The second half of my Portugal weekend: an evening and a full day in Porto after a 2.5-hour, €6 bus ride from Lisbon. I LOVED the traditional Portuguese ceramic tiles! Gorgeous!

MonacoB TIER

Monaco

Mar 14 through 16

A day across the border for the Monaco F1 Inside Track tour and a private walking tour. So cool to see this in person!

SpainB TIER

Segovia

Day trip from Madrid

An easy day trip, about an hour from madrid via bus ride. This castle inspired the creation of the Disney castle which I thought was so awesome!

SpainB TIER

Ávila

Day trip from Madrid

About 90 minutes from Madrid, this walled medieval city made for a rewarding day trip. The city was so peaceful.

FranceC TIER

Nice

Mar 14 through 16

Truffle ravioli at Le Grand Balcon and gelato at Fenocchio. It was just cold and slightly more crowded then expected. I wish I was able to walk on the beach without being hurt by rocks.

PortugalC TIER

Lisbon

Feb 20 through 22

I love the atmosphere in Lisbon! Pastel de Natas were great! It just felt a lot like San Francisco with the enormous hills!

NetherlandsD TIER

Amsterdam

Feb 13 through 16

Paired with Brussels but I was not able to see Amsterdam during the day. It was EXTREMELY cold. We took a canal cruise and tried Dutch crepes.
